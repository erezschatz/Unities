# The essay says whose idea this is

## Context

From a conversation with Erez on 2026-11-19, after the repository was opened.

Erez said that the unities being a French rule attributed backwards to Aristotle is,
ironically, important to him, because he saw the Unities as making the same move toward
software: "you had ideas in software development, and what we're creating here is
basically saying, no, this is what they meant. This is what XP or Agile meant."

The answer given to him: the neoclassical claim was false as history, and the parallel
claim is false too. XP wanted less written record, not more; the Unities share its aim
and reverse its rule. The claim that holds is "this is what their aim requires now". He
agreed that this was what he meant, and said to put it in the essay, which he will read
at home and judge.

The two paragraphs were drafted by Claude, not by Erez. The essay is his.

**Promise** — A reader who knows XP or the history of the dramatic unities finds the
essay has already said what they were about to object.

## What was done

Two paragraphs were added to `docs/the-unities.md`, at the end of "Where this comes
from". The first says that Aristotle argues only for unity of action, mentions time once
and never place, and that the rule of three was made in the sixteenth and seventeenth
centuries, by Castelvetro and then the French, under his name. The second says that XP
and the agile manifesto saw the same problem and answered it by keeping knowledge in
people who sit together, that the Unities say the opposite, and that the claim is
therefore not "this is what they meant" but "this is what their aim requires now".

The historical statements were written from general knowledge and not checked against
sources here: Castelvetro's commentary on the *Poetics* (1570), Aristotle's one remark on
time, "a promise of a conversation" for a story card, and the manifesto's line on working
software and documentation.

Nothing else changed. The plugin is still 0.5.5: the essay is not part of the procedure.
